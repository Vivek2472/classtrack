What ClassTrack does

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

Built around the exact pain points of engineering attendance — not generic task management.

event_repeat
Per-Session Tracking

Log each occurrence of a subject independently. Two Physics lectures on the same day count as two separate attendance events, not one.

calendar_month
Smart Weekly Schedule

Visual timetable with color-coded theory, lab, and tutorial slots. See your entire week at a glance and know exactly what's coming.

analytics
Live Attendance Analytics

Subject-wise breakdown, type-wise stats (theory vs lab vs tutorial), and a live overall aggregate — everything in real time.

how_to_reg
Quick Mark Attendance

One-tap Present / Absent / Cancelled logging from anywhere. A prominent button always at the top — never more than a second away.

cloud_done
Cloud Database Sync

Instant PostgreSQL database persistence with secure Row Level Security. Seamless multi-device sync and automatic real-time backups.
